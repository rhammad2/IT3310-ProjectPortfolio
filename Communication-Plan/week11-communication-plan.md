## Communication Matrix

**Stakeholder:** RCHN Compliance Officer

## Communication Method Justification

**Entry 2:** I chose Push for the Chief Medical Officer because they need regular updates about patient safety and project progress. Sending an executive summary helps them stay informed.

**Entry 3:** I chose Interactive for Clinic Intake Coordinators because they need to understand workflow changes and training. This allows them to ask questions and share concerns.

---

## Escalation Path

**Level 1 – Developer:** Handles technical problems. Triggered when the system has an issue. Recommended response time: 2 hours.

**Level 2 – Project Manager:** Handles problems the developer cannot solve or that could delay the project. Recommended response time: 4 hours.

**Level 3 – Marcus Webb:** Makes decisions about major delays, budget changes, or additional resources. Recommended response time: 24 hours.

These response times are my planning recommendations.

## Escalation Scenario

**Risk:** Appointment data errors from the Week 9 Risk Register.

**Step 1:** The Developer finds appointment data errors and tries to fix them within 2 hours.

**Step 2:** If the problem cannot be fixed or could delay the project, the Developer informs the Project Manager. The Project Manager reviews it within 4 hours.

**Step 3:** If the problem affects the launch date or needs more resources, the Project Manager contacts Marcus Webb within 24 hours for a decision.

## Gap Analysis

### Communication Gap

The gap I found is that Clinic Intake Coordinators only receive weekly updates. If an important workflow change happens, they might not know right away. This could cause confusion and mistakes with patient appointments.

To fix this, I would add immediate updates whenever an important workflow change happens.

### Silence as a Communication Decision

If NovaTech does not send regular updates, Marcus Webb might think the project is delayed or having problems, even if everything is going as planned.

My communication plan prevents this by providing weekly meetings to share progress, discuss risks, and answer questions.
