# User Persona and Product Vision: RCHN Patient Intake and Scheduling System

**Consultant:** Raian Hammad  
**Role:** Associate Consultant, Product Manager, NovaTech Consulting  
**Engagement:** Riverside Community Health Network (RCHN)  
**Date:** September 22, 2026

---

## User Persona

**Name:** Maria  
**Role:** Intake Coordinator  
**Years in Role:** 3–7 years  
**Tech Comfort Level:** Medium, comfortable with common tools

### Primary Work Goal
Complete patient registration quickly and correctly.

### Main Frustration
The current system uses too many screens to complete one patient registration.

### What Would Make Their Work Better
A simpler system that lets her complete patient registration with fewer screens.

---

## Product Vision Statement

For clinic intake coordinators who need a simpler and more consistent way to complete patient registration and scheduling, a unified patient intake and scheduling system will make the process easier and more reliable across RCHN clinics.

---

## Product Vision Reflection

I chose intake coordinators because they use the system for patient registration and scheduling. The RCHN research shows that they have to use too many screens to complete one patient registration. A simpler and more consistent system can make their work easier and more reliable. This can also help RCHN improve the intake and scheduling process.
