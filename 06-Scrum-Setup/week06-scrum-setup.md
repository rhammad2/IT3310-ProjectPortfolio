
# Scrum Setup Document: RCHN Patient Intake and Scheduling System

**Consultant:** Raian Hammad  
**Role:** Associate Consultant, NovaTech Consulting  
**Engagement:** Riverside Community Health Network (RCHN)

## Agile Delivery Fit

Agile works well for RCHN because the team can improve the system in small steps and get feedback from clinic staff. This helps the team find problems early and make changes. However, security, patient data, and system connections still need careful planning.

## Scrum Team

**Product Owner:** RCHN representative responsible for product priorities.

**Scrum Master:** NovaTech Scrum Master.

**Developers:** NovaTech IT and development team.

### Rationale

The Product Owner decides which work is most important for RCHN. The Scrum Master helps the team follow Scrum and remove problems. Developers build and test the system.

## Sprint 1 Plan

**Sprint Goal:** Prepare the main scheduling and intake workflows and review existing data for possible problems.

**Course Planning Limit:** 25 points

### Selected Backlog Items

- Scheduling Workflow Prototype, High Priority, 13 points
- Intake Form Standardization, High Priority, 8 points
- Legacy Data Cleanup Review, Low Priority, 3 points

**Total Course Planning Points Selected:** 24 points

## Sprint Planning Reflection

I selected these items because they support the main goal of improving patient intake and scheduling. The scheduling prototype helps the team review how appointments will work. Intake form standardization helps make the process consistent across all six clinics. Legacy data cleanup helps identify problems with existing information. Together, these tasks use 24 of the 25 available points.

I decided to wait on dashboard reporting because the team should focus on the main intake and scheduling work first. The team also needs feedback from clinic staff before moving forward.
