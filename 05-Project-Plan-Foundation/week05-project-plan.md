# Project Plan Foundation: RCHN Patient Intake and Scheduling System

**Consultant:** Raian Hammad  
**Role:** Associate Consultant, Project Manager, NovaTech Consulting  
**Engagement:** Riverside Community Health Network (RCHN)  
**Date:** October 6, 2026

---

## Scope

### In Scope, Phase One
- Patient intake workflow standardization across all six clinics
- Appointment scheduling system replacement
- Staff training for the new system at all six locations
- Initial post-launch rollout support

### Out of Scope, Phase One
- Patient-facing online scheduling portal
- Billing system integration with the new scheduling data
- Executive reporting dashboard for clinic leadership
- Mobile application for clinic staff

### Scope Justification
I recommend excluding the mobile application from phase one because the main focus is replacing the intake and scheduling system. Adding a mobile app would increase the work, cost, resources, and project complexity.

---

## SMART Objectives

### Objective 1
By the end of the approved project, RCHN will use a consistent patient intake and scheduling workflow across all six clinics.

**Connection to Product Vision:** This supports the product vision by making intake and scheduling more consistent across all six clinics.

### Objective 2
By the approved rollout milestone, staff at all six RCHN clinics will be prepared to use the new intake and scheduling system.

**Connection to Product Vision:** This supports the product vision by helping staff use the new system reliably.

---

## Planning Constraints

### Constraint 1
The project must address all six RCHN clinics.

**Effect on Planning:** This affects resources and scheduling because the project must consider all six locations.

### Constraint 2
Phase One focuses on the authorized intake and scheduling system replacement.

**Effect on Planning:** This affects scope because additional features must be evaluated before being added.

---

## Assumptions

### Assumption 1
We are assuming that staff at all six clinics will be available for training. If this is wrong, the training schedule and rollout plan may need to change.

### Assumption 2
We are assuming that the six clinics will be able to follow the same intake and scheduling workflow. If this is wrong, the scope and system requirements may need to change.

---

## Planning Foundation Synthesis

One planning decision I made was to include patient intake and scheduling across all six clinics in the project scope. My Week 2 Project Charter helped me make this decision because it identified the need to replace the current system. I had to decide what should be included in phase one and what should wait until later. The final scope still needs confirmation from the project sponsor.
