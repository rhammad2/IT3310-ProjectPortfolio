## Engagement Strategy Reflection

### Reflection Part 1
The RCHN Chief Medical Officer requires the most attention because they have high interest and high influence. They should stay involved in important project decisions. If their concerns are overlooked, the project may not meet RCHN's clinical needs.

### Reflection Part 2
I did not include patients in my stakeholder register because they were not listed in the RCHN stakeholder snapshot. I would add them later if the project collects patient feedback or shows that patients need to be directly involved in the new system.
