Marcus Webb

---

## Business Need

RCHN needs to replace its outdated patient intake and scheduling system used across six clinics. This project matters because RCHN needs a reliable system that can support more consistent intake and scheduling processes. NovaTech must confirm the specific needs with clinic staff and leaders before detailed planning begins.

---

## Purpose and High-Level Scope

The purpose of this project is to replace RCHN’s outdated patient intake and scheduling system with a more reliable system for its six clinics. The high-level scope includes reviewing current processes, gathering information from stakeholders, identifying the clinics’ needs, and establishing the main direction for the replacement project.

---

## Success Criteria

1. The replacement system supports patient intake and scheduling across all six clinics.
2. Clinic managers and front desk staff confirm that the system supports their main work needs.
3. The system provides a consistent and reliable intake and scheduling process.
